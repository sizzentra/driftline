# 🎣 Driftline: my full game review
Went through the whole game piece by piece. Big picture: **systems first, UI polish last.** A lot of menus look rough, but there's no point polishing screens for systems that are still changing. Everything's saved in the plan word for word. Read through, and tell me what you agree with, what you'd change and what I missed. 🙏

## 🐟 Fishing
**Too much pop-up text in fights**
- "Nice!", "Steady!", call-outs and tips stack up around the fish
- It hand-holds instead of letting you *read* the fish and learn the fight, and that kills the fun for me
- Fewer words, more feel: the fish's movement, the line and the sounds should tell you what's going on

**Fight pace**
- With no upgrades, nothing should feel instant or too easy
- But when a fight drags and the fish visually crawls in, it feels like a slow escort quest, not a tough battle
- Fights should be hard because they're **tense** (tension, timing, reading the fish), not because they're **slow**

*(The option to switch back to the old fight display is already on the test builds: Settings › Display › Fight display.)*

## 📈 Progression
**Too fast and shallow once you're built up**
- Prestige only needs Hollow Trench open (the 12th water)
- Each water opens after **10 catches** at the one before, plus coins (10 more catches each voyage, up to 50)
- Brand-new player: coins hold you back, about **1,180 catches** to reach Hollow Trench
- With built-up bonuses: fish pay about **100x more**, prices stop mattering, and you're at Hollow Trench in about **190 catches**
- Not a grind, but unlocking should depend on **progress at each water** (its fish, oddities, bounties), not "catch 10 fish and have coins"

**Upgrade numbers feel tiny**
- A 5% upgrade doesn't feel like anything; 15–25% steps do
- Why is billions of coins the ceiling? Bigger numbers are fine (even e26 someday) **if the balance holds**. Open question: how

**No real builds**
- Skills don't work together, so the only "builds" are shiny or junk
- Some skills are clearly way better than others
- The max-rank bonus idea is great, keep it

**Paths are too narrow**
- Every fork should be a real decision; right now lots are obvious picks

**Saved loadouts** ⭐
- Min-maxers swap gear, Paths, skills, bait and pets across a bunch of menus every time they switch between shiny hunting, Fish Log and oddity chasing
- Save a whole setup and switch in one click: huge quality-of-life win

**(3/6)**
## 🌊 Prestige
- **Reset most things.** Carrying everything over doesn't feel right; permanent progress comes from feats and earned rewards *(skill points from feats and the Fish Log already carry over on the test builds)*
- **Wisdom Pearl formula** doesn't feel good
- **Boons** are a great idea, but earning them has no direction
- **Relics need balancing.** Some are no-brainers, and because billions of coins come so easily, you pick up way too many too fast
- **Expensive rewards should feel like rewards.** Junk chance is useless if you don't want junk
- Better choices that aren't so narrowly targeted, maybe **unlocking new features or systems**? Not sure yet

## 🏆 Challenges
**Drop daily tasks and weekly quests**
- Hard to make good, and they don't add much
- Make **bounties** the main thing to chase instead: deeper, more rewarding, **rewards you can choose**, and easy to notice (right now they're easy to overlook)
- ⚠️ This pulls against your 5 daily / 10 weekly challenges per water idea. Let's settle it together

---
**(4/6)**
**Feats should be the real challenges**
- **No coins as rewards** (right now even "Land 10 fish" pays 100 coins)
- Every goal has to say clearly what to do. Some make no sense ("catch a falling star"?)
- Better rewards, and they're the natural home for **permanent account progress**
- Bring back the **feat groups from the live build** (Catching, Collection, Legends & myths, Pets…), each with a progress bar and a **"Finish all" bonus forever** like +5% bite speed. The bonuses still work on the test builds, but the clear group list got lost in a menu rework. Bring it back with more depth

**Tide Pass needs a real rework**
- It's unengaging for what it should be
- Better rewards, or move its depth into better feats
- **Scales** are hard to earn, and the game never says where they come from

**Events, Challenges tab and Derby**
- The event calendar is great but needs **more variety**, something players actually want to watch for
- A tab called "Challenges" inside the Challenges menu is confusing, and most of those challenges need love and expansion
- The **Derby** is too easy, feels out of place and has zero depth
- If we add **co-op** down the road, that's where a lot of this could really shine

**(5/6)**
## 🗺️ Waters & fish
- **Fish should belong to their water.** A lot don't match their environment's theme
- **More meme fish**, and we approve every meme fish's name, description and card picture (not every fish, just the memes)
- **Waters should play differently**, not just look different. Right now a new water is mostly new scenery

## 📚 Collecting
- We love collecting (Pokémon brain 🧠)
- **Oddities and treasures can't really be chased.** Players don't know how to find them and struggle to get any. On live it's an extreme chase
- One big pool is okay, but maybe split it up with a creative idea so you can work toward specific ones
- **No permanent home for fish.** After a prestige there's nowhere to keep favourites on purpose. The Trophy Room was a placeholder and could become a **record log**

## 🧊 Cooler, Aquarium & Nibbles
- The Cooler could be organized more cleanly
- Inspect is solid, but more info could show without opening it
- Sell vs aquarium is an easy call (good)
- **Scrapping** is under-explained and feels outside the loop, even though Nibbles are a great idea
- Nibbles (grow the aquarium) and duplicate catches (Fish Log mastery) need more nuance and better progression layers

---
**(6/6)**
## 🐾 Everything else
- **Pets:** great, but levelling should be more than tapping. Going all in on pets could unlock global buffs, with clear goals like feats
- **Boardwalk:** chance games are fun but shouldn't push your progress ahead. Needs a purpose besides coins and materials, maybe **rerolls**? Games should feel more fun and intentional (still undecided)
- **Customizing:** love it. Some pieces look off, and we need funnier options
- **Music:** cozy, but some loops get repetitive over a long session
- **Weather & events:** better visuals first, plus an easy way to see what's active, for how long and what it does. Locking fish to weather would be a pain unless there's a really good idea
- **Teaching the game:** show the **real formulas**, no pop-up tips, and the Quests tab should be clean, thorough and **100% accurate**. The fishing feel is a skill you learn, not something the UI hands you
- **Saving:** about 4 save systems and download options side by side. Make it one simple path
- **Phones:** the header and side panel around the fishing scene are great; the insides of menus need work. PC needs its own headers and fishing scene since it has way more room
- **UI overall:** rough in a lot of places, but it gets polished **last**
