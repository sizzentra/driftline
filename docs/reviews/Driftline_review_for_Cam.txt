DRIFTLINE: MY FULL GAME REVIEW
==================================================
I went through the whole game piece by piece. Big picture: SYSTEMS FIRST,
UI POLISH LAST. A lot of menus look rough, but there's no point polishing
screens for systems that are still changing. Everything here is also saved
in the plan word for word. Read through it, and tell me what you agree
with, what you'd change and what I missed.


--------------------------------------------------
1. FISHING
--------------------------------------------------
Too much pop-up text in fights
- "Nice!", "Steady!", call-outs and tips stack up around the fish.
- It hand-holds instead of letting you read the fish and learn the fight,
  and that takes away the fun for me.
- Fewer words, more feel: the fish's movement, the line and the sounds
  should tell you what's going on.

Fight pace
- With no upgrades, nothing should feel instant or too easy.
- But when a fight drags and the fish visually crawls in, it feels like a
  slow escort quest, not a tough battle.
- Fights should be hard because they're TENSE (tension, timing, reading
  the fish), not because they're SLOW.

(Already done on the test builds: the option to switch back to the old
fight display, in Settings > Display > Fight display.)


--------------------------------------------------
2. PROGRESSION
--------------------------------------------------
Too fast and shallow once you're built up
- Prestige only needs Hollow Trench open (the 12th water).
- Each water opens after 10 catches at the one before, plus coins
  (10 more catches each voyage, up to 50).
- Brand-new player: coins hold you back, about 1,180 catches to reach
  Hollow Trench.
- With built-up bonuses: fish pay about 100x more, prices stop mattering,
  and you're at Hollow Trench in about 190 catches.
- Not a grind, but unlocking should depend on progress at each water
  (its fish, oddities, bounties), not "catch 10 fish and have coins".
  (Numbers checked in the test build, counting fish sales only.)

Upgrade numbers feel tiny
- A 5% upgrade doesn't feel like anything; 15-25% steps do.
- Why is billions of coins the ceiling? Bigger numbers are fine (even e26
  someday) if the balance holds. Open question: how.

No real builds
- Skills don't work together, so the only "builds" are shiny or junk.
- Some skills are clearly way better than others.
- The max-rank bonus idea is great, keep it.

Paths are too narrow
- Every fork should be a real decision; right now lots are obvious picks.

Saved loadouts (big one)
- Min-maxers swap gear, Paths, skills, bait and pets across a bunch of
  menus every time they switch between shiny hunting, the Fish Log and
  oddity chasing.
- Save a whole setup and switch in one click: a huge quality-of-life win.


--------------------------------------------------
3. PRESTIGE
--------------------------------------------------
- Reset most things. Carrying everything over doesn't feel right;
  permanent progress comes from feats and earned rewards.
  (Already done on the test builds: skill points from feats and the Fish
  Log carry over.)
- The Wisdom Pearl formula doesn't feel good.
- Boons are a great idea, but earning them has no direction.
- Relics need balancing. Some are no-brainers, and because billions of
  coins come so easily, you pick up way too many too fast.
- Expensive rewards should feel like rewards. Junk chance is useless if
  you don't want junk.
- Better choices that aren't so narrowly targeted, maybe unlocking new
  features or systems? Not sure yet.


--------------------------------------------------
4. CHALLENGES
--------------------------------------------------
Drop daily tasks and weekly quests
- They're hard to make good, and they don't add much.
- Make BOUNTIES the main thing to chase instead: deeper, more rewarding,
  with rewards you can choose, and easy to notice (right now they're easy
  to overlook).
- !! This pulls against your 5 daily / 10 weekly challenges per water idea.
  Let's settle it together.

Feats should be the real challenges
- No coins as rewards (right now even "Land 10 fish" pays 100 coins).
- Every goal has to say clearly what to do. Some make no sense
  ("catch a falling star"?).
- Better rewards, and they're the natural home for permanent account
  progress.
- Bring back the feat groups from the live build (Catching, Collection,
  Legends & myths, Pets...), each with a progress bar and a "Finish all"
  bonus that lasts forever, like +5% bite speed. The bonuses still work on
  the test builds, but the clear group list got lost in a menu rework.
  Bring it back with more depth.

Tide Pass needs a real rework
- It's unengaging for what it should be.
- Better rewards, or move its depth into better feats.
- Scales are hard to earn, and the game never says where they come from.

Events, the Challenges tab and the Derby
- The event calendar is great but needs more variety, something players
  actually want to watch for.
- A tab called "Challenges" inside the Challenges menu is confusing, and
  most of those challenges need love and expansion.
- The Derby is too easy, feels out of place and has zero depth.
- If we add co-op down the road, that's where a lot of this could really
  shine.


--------------------------------------------------
5. WATERS & FISH
--------------------------------------------------
- Fish should belong to their water. A lot don't match their
  environment's theme.
- More meme fish, and we approve every meme fish's name, description and
  card picture (not every fish, just the memes).
- Waters should play differently, not just look different. Right now a
  new water is mostly new scenery.


--------------------------------------------------
6. COLLECTING
--------------------------------------------------
- We love collecting (a lot of us grew up on Pokemon).
- Oddities and treasures can't really be chased. Players don't know how to
  find them and struggle to get any; on live it's an extreme chase.
- One big pool is okay, but maybe split it up with a creative idea so you
  can work toward specific ones.
- No permanent home for fish. After a prestige there's nowhere to keep
  favourites on purpose. The Trophy Room was a placeholder and could
  become a record log.


--------------------------------------------------
7. COOLER, AQUARIUM & NIBBLES
--------------------------------------------------
- The Cooler could be organized more cleanly.
- Inspect is solid, but more information could show without opening it.
- Choosing between selling and the aquarium is easy (good).
- Scrapping is under-explained and feels outside the loop, even though
  Nibbles are a great idea.
- Nibbles (they grow the aquarium) and duplicate catches (Fish Log
  mastery) need more nuance and better progression layers.


--------------------------------------------------
8. EVERYTHING ELSE
--------------------------------------------------
Pets
- Great, but levelling should be more than tapping. Going all in on pets
  could unlock global buffs and rewards, with clear goals like feats.

Boardwalk
- Chance games are fun, but they shouldn't push your progress ahead.
- They need a purpose besides coins and materials, maybe rerolls?
- The games themselves should feel more fun and intentional.
  (Still undecided.)

Customizing
- Love it. Some pieces look off, and we need funnier options.

Music
- Cozy, but some loops are short and get repetitive over a long session.

Weather & events
- Better visuals first.
- An easy way to see what's active, for how long and what it does.
- Locking fish to specific weather would be a pain unless there's a
  really good idea for it.

Teaching the game
- Show players the real formulas, so they can judge what's good for the
  way they like to play.
- No pop-up tips.
- The Quests tab should be cleaned up, thorough and above all 100%
  accurate.
- The fishing feel is a skill you learn over time, not something the
  interface hands you.

Saving
- About 4 save systems and download options sit side by side, never
  organized. Make it one simple path a player understands at a glance.

Phones
- The top header and side panel around the fishing scene are great.
- The insides of the menus need work.
- PC needs its own headers and fishing scene, since it has way more room.

UI overall
- Rough in a lot of places, but it gets polished LAST, once the systems
  above are done.
