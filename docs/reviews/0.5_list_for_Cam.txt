B. FISHING AND FIGHT FEEL, BY ROUND
B1 Fight rework: fish must be tired out, holding through a rare fish's runs snaps the line, jumps have a warning, second winds.
B2 [SWITCH] Your fight feel: fish rest between moves, weight decides what a move can do, 40 m casts, 10 s without reeling lets it go.
B3 Your feel update: tension builds while reeling, temper, fish swim toward you, more second winds for rare fish.
B4 Middle feel: warnings before moves, right reactions pay, perfect pull, combo, in the groove, darting and long runs, big-catch fanfare.
B5 Rounds 3 and 4: full temper punishes reeling, snap after a third of a second at 100%, close-hooked rare fish surge, chains, line out at 80 m.
B6 Round 5: tired fish much stronger than your tackle come in slowly; 1 second no-cast after Fish Frenzy.
B7 Round 6: tired fish keep half their hold; shiny, titled and hybrid fish fight harder; fish get harder each voyage up to 100.
B8 Round 7: every fish kind has its own hold and stamina, your strength reels weaker fish faster, temper hits harder, fish stronger everywhere (to about 300 lb).
B9 Dives in deep waters: keep the tension in the pink band to cut a dive short.
B10 Bites: one try per bite, skills add tries; miss them all at a hotspot and you lose bait and streak.
B11 Hotspot colours show the lowest rarity there; a sparkling hotspot promises a shiny.
B12 Swallowed catches: the big fish pulls line steadily, at most about 50 m; the swallowed fish always goes in the cooler.
B13 Oddities reel in faster but snag sooner.
B14 [WORK] Quiet Waters: no pop-ups on the fishing screen for weather, events or challenges.
B15 Short fight cues and hints.
B16 Fight signs around the fish (tension ring, instruction above it), on PC too.
B17 Travel while resting.
B18 Streak XP climbs half as fast, same top.
Note: the B2 switch turns B2 to B9 off together and gives the "Old" fight of the test builds. Single rounds cannot be switched. B8 is the round that made top-water fights much harder, which you and the director still have to settle.
