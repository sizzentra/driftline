## Build 1929: the blur's real cause, and true reflections (2026-11-23 night)

ChatGPT's dusk and night paintings are separately painted: their outlines match the day painting about 97.5%, and the fine detail (petals, branches) sits in slightly different places. Measured: the three paintings are equally sharp on their own (detail per contrast 0.48 / 0.47 / 0.47), so the blend was the cause.
