# Driftline home designs (batch 5)

Every water's home, designed from its entry in `docs/MAP_THEMES.md` (place, culture, palette, materials). Written 2026-11-22; the director approved the Willow Pond pilot and asked for all homes to be built.

## The format (every home)
- **6 features: the seat on every home, plus 5 features made for that water from its description** (director, 2026-11-22: "besides chairs, the other 5 options for each map should be created based on that map's description"). No two waters need share the other five; Willow Pond (the approved pilot) keeps its own: light, deck, posts, rail, canopy.
- **3 styles per feature,** each a real choice that fits the water (a feature may also offer "none" first, then 3 real styles).
- **One colour list per home, 16 colours** (MAP_THEMES › that water's "Home colours"), shared by every feature.
- **The seat:** resting sits the angler in it. It stands on the home at the seat spot.
- **A glow at night:** each home has a feature that carries its light (marked **(light)** below); its glow, light pool and reflection are drawn live, like the pilot's lantern.
- **Same footprint, standing surface, angler and pet spots on every home;** side view only; supports run into the water with the water flowing past them; floating homes bob; Frostbite stands on ice; Starfall floats; Circuit Lake hovers.
- **Defaults** are the first style of every feature, in colours close to today's home.

Each feature: **style 1 · style 2 · style 3** (each a short side silhouette).

## Music rework (batch 5, director's request)
**Found:** the music is made live in the browser (no audio files), one theme per water. Only 10 waters have their own theme. The other 10 borrow one, and several don't fit their water at all: Willow Pond and Stonebrook River use the general lo-fi and acoustic themes; Cherry Blossom Lake uses Frostbite's music box; the Canadian Rockies and Corporate Pond use Brine Shelf's harbour strings; Florida Keys and The Buffet use Coral Shallows' island theme; **Amazon uses Ember's hot-spring drums**, **The Nile uses Midnight Deep's storm cello**, **Circuit Lake uses Hollow Trench's deep dreams**, Animal Farm uses Saltmarsh's sea shanty, and Mount Olympus uses Starfall's starlight.

**Plan:** every water gets its own theme, built from its "Music and ambience" line in MAP_THEMES, with its own instruments made from the same live sound tools, a day version and a calmer night version, and its own ambience layer. The existing themes are kept where they already fit (shanty → Saltmarsh, music box → Frostbite, island → Coral Shallows, harbour strings → Brine Shelf, storm cello → Midnight Deep, deep dreams → Hollow Trench, hot-spring drums → Ember, starlight → Starfall) and improved to match their water.
