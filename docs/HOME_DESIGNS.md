# Driftline home designs (batch 5)

Every water's home, designed from its entry in `docs/MAP_THEMES.md` (place, culture, palette, materials). Written 2026-11-22 at the director's request: all homes and their options designed up front.

**Status: designs, waiting for the director's approval.** Nothing here is drawn yet. The side-view step in `docs/HOME_STANDARD.md` applies: each style below is a one-line side silhouette, the director approves (or changes) it, and only then is it drawn and judged against its line.

**Build order (recommended):** first the Willow Pond pilot rework (from the director's notes) to settle the format, then the other 19 in one go. Music rework at the end of this file.

## The format (every home)
- **6 features:** the **light** and the **seat** on every home, plus **4 features that fit that home** (a dock has deck, supports, rail, canopy; a boat has hull, cabin, mast and so on). Decorations no longer exist; these 6 features are all the player customises.
- **3 styles per feature,** each a real choice that fits the water.
- **One colour list per home, 16 colours,** shared by every feature (MAP_THEMES › that water's "Home colours"), so parts can match.
- **The seat:** resting sits the angler in it; pets sit on or beside it (Pet Nap). It stands on the deck at the angler's spot.
- **Side view, standing on the deck, supports into the water** with a subtle flow (floating homes bob instead; Frostbite stands on ice; Circuit Lake hovers, Starfall floats: their themes).
- **Default:** the first style of every feature and colours chosen to look like today's home, so nothing jumps when the new home arrives.

Each feature lists: **style 1 · style 2 · style 3**, each as its side silhouette.
