# Cam's fix list (received 2026-11-21)
Project 2 of the overnight roadmap (PLAN › Production queue). Cam's words, numbered for reference; Claude's notes in *italics*.

1. **Scrap text:** when scrapping a fish, the text mentions the tier of Nibble, not the fish.
2. **Water portraits** take about 10 seconds to load after a reset.
3. **Florida Keys:** a speedboat goes by in the background now and then; more life. The bridge in the back better and larger; connected roads between the keys with cars, minivans and the occasional box truck or semi that fade off screen. Sport fishing is huge: a few fishing boats rip by too. Schooling fish visible under the water. Beaches visible. Upgrade and enhance: "go full Florida with the quality". *(Goes with Living Maps, side-view design step first.)*
4. **Amazon Rainforest:** more insects with better models flying by (dragonflies, butterflies, etc.). Fix the high bridge and where the home dock is drawn. Roots and other water foliage. Channels stay thin but detailed. Keep the waterfall with better topography around it. Tropical birds, water snakes in the water, reptiles and amphibians big and small, some bright, some stealthy (lizards, alligators, bullfrogs, dart frogs). Occasional floating branches and debris. Deep rainforest brush visible. *(Goes with Living Maps, side-view design step first.)*
5. **Running fish turn fast:** when a fish runs it snaps much quicker to the other direction; no noticeable flipping animation. *(Goes with Fight Feel.)*
6. **Wrong badges:** unlock indicators sometimes show wrong numbers (Bounties shows 1 with nothing to turn in).
7. **Scrap all beside Sell all** in the cooler, with matching buttons.
8. **Cooler sort by Rarity** (a new sort option).
9. **Feeding Nibbles makes the screen jitter** on phones.
10. **Hook attempts over hotspots (skill rework):** no multiple hook attempts as a baseline. Start with 1 attempt over a hotspot; skills grant extra hotspot attempts before the bait is used up and the streak breaks. Outside hotspots, a missed hook does not end the streak but scales with extra attempts the same way. Missing all your attempts over a hotspot breaks the streak and uses up the bait: streak-broken text "You waited too long, the fish got your bait!" *(Balance: Claude builds a first version, the director and Cam set the numbers.)*
11. **Imperial / metric toggles** for cast distance and water depth, rounded so they read evenly ("about" for water depth). Stored in today's units: water in feet, cast distance in metres, fish weight in lb/oz.
12. **Endgame waters stay "Uncharted Waters"** everywhere until their voyage is unlocked; their Waters tile says "Complete Voyage X to unlock" instead of "Unlock X coins". The coin price still applies after that.
13. **Hollow Trench** lists at 3000 m+ depth and its fish are on average stronger than Midnight Deep's. *(Director, 2026-11-21: "3000ft+". Claude's correction, same day: the game actually stores every depth in metres (b_data.js / cm_waters.js), not feet; Midnight Deep reads "500–3,000 m" and Hollow Trench "1,000 m +". 3,000 ft is about 900 m, shallower than Midnight Deep, Re-confirmed by the director: **"3,000 m+"**. **Dives need a water at least 100 m deep** (director, 2026-11-21; replaces "100 ft"); depths stay stored in metres (the metric/imperial switch converts for display). Same note for the dive rule's "deeper than 100 ft" (about 30 m) and item 11's "water measured in feet".)*
