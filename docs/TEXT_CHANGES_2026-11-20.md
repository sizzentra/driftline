# Text changes, 2026-11-20 (test builds; for the director and Cam to judge)

Jokes are unchanged. Fish Log descriptions are unchanged. “Phones” and “PC” show where a line differs.

## Fight cues (on the water, every build)
| Moment | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| About to jump | Get ready to let go! | It’s jumping! |
| Jumping | It jumped! Let go! | Let go! |
| Head shake | Head shake! Ease off | Head shake! |
| Line straining | The line is straining! Ease off (PC) / Line strained! Ease off (phones) | Ease off! |

## Fishing hints
| When | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Cooler full | Cooler full. Open the Cooler tab to sell, or move fish to the Aquarium. | Cooler full. Sell in the Cooler or move fish to the Aquarium. |
| Hotspot | A hotspot is rippling! Cast right into it for rarer fish. | A hotspot! Cast into it for rarer fish. |
| Aim cast (phones) | Tap the water where you want to cast. | Tap the water to cast there. |
| Aim cast (PC) | Click the water where you want to cast (or hold Space for the power meter). | Click the water to cast there (hold Space for power). |
| Power cast (phones) | Touch and hold the water to charge a cast, let go to throw. | Touch and hold to charge, let go to cast. |
| Power cast (PC) | Hold on the water (or Space) to charge a cast, release to throw. | Hold on the water (or Space) to charge, release to cast. |
| Charging | Let go / Release to cast. A far cast or a hotspot improves your odds. | Let go / Release to cast. Far casts and hotspots bring better fish. |
| Waiting | Watch the bobber. Small twitches are nibbles: wait for the big splash. | Twitches are nibbles. Wait for the big splash. |
| Bite (phones) | Now! Tap the water to set the hook. | Now! Tap to hook it. |
| Bite (PC) | Now! Click or press Space to set the hook. | Now! Click or Space to hook it. |
| Reeling | Hold to reel, or draw circles to crank. Let go when it runs, jumps, shakes or gets angry. | Hold or draw circles to reel. Let go on runs, jumps, shakes and anger. |
| Frenzy (PC) | Tap the leaping fish! | Click the leaping fish! |
